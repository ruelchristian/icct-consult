## Slide 1: The Problem & Our Solution (1 Minute)

### What to say:
> "Good day everyone! Today, I am presenting the **Web-Based Faculty Consultation and Scheduling System** for ICCT Colleges.
> 
> Before this system:
> - Students waited in long lines outside faculty rooms without knowing if the professor was free.
> - Scheduling was done manually, causing double bookings and missed appointments.
> - Consultation records were written on paper and often lost.
> 
> Our solution is a fast, easy-to-use web and mobile portal that lets students book consultations in seconds, helps professors manage their weekly hours, and gives the school official reports."

---

## Slide 2: For Students — Fast & Easy Booking (1 Minute)

### What to say:
> "For students, everything takes just 3 simple steps:
> 1. **Browse Faculty:** Search for professors by name or department.
> 2. **Pick a Time:** Click on the calendar to see available 30-minute slots. No double-booking is allowed.
> 3. **Submit Request:** Choose Face-to-Face or Online, type the reason, and book.
> 
> Students receive instant notifications and can install the app on their phone like a regular mobile app (PWA)."

---

## Slide 3: For Faculty — Smart Schedule Management (1 Minute)

### What to say:
> "For professors:
> - **Set Weekly Hours:** Professors can add their weekly consultation hours (e.g. Monday 9 to 11 AM) in one click.
> - **Accept or Decline:** They can quickly accept incoming student requests.
> - **Record Advice:** After the meeting, professors record attendance and write advice for the student.
> - **Privacy First:** Professors have a private notes section that students can never see. Only the professor and dean can read it."

---

## Slide 4: For Department Heads — Reports & PDF Export (1 Minute)

### What to say:
> "For department heads and deans:
> - **Live Dashboard:** See real-time numbers of total bookings, completed consultations, and no-shows.
> - **1-Click PDF Export:** Download an official ICCT consultation report with one click, ready for school audits and accreditation.
> - **Zero Paperwork:** Everything is stored safely in the database."

---

## Slide 5: System Highlights & Conclusion (1 Minute)

### What to say:
> "In summary, here is what makes our system strong:
> - **Works on Any Device:** Works smoothly on desktop, tablet, and mobile phones with zero screen overflow.
> - **Installable App (PWA):** Works like a phone app with offline support.
> - **Secure & Tested:** We ran 11 automated black-box tests across all user flows with 100% success.
> 
> Thank you! I am now ready for your questions or a live walkthrough."
